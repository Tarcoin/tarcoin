⛏️ New Miner Setup Guide
Step-by-Step · ASIC Mining

✅ What You Need Before Starting

⚙️ SHA-256 ASIC miner (Antminer S19/S21, Whatsminer M50/M60, Avalonminer A12+)
🔌 Power supply unit + stable internet connection (ethernet recommended)
💻 A computer, tablet, or phone to set up your wallet
❌ You do NOT need GitHub, coding skills, or technical knowledge
1
Get a TAR Wallet Address

Download the official Tarcoin wallet from the website. This generates your personal TAR address where mining rewards are sent.

Download from
https://tarcoin.org/download
Your OSFile to Download
Windowstarcoin-qt-win64.exe
macOStarcoin-qt-macos.dmg
Linuxtarcoin-qt-linux.tar.gz

Install it → Open it → Your wallet address is automatically generated and shown on screen. It will look like:

tar1qt509uycayxx9t55hfkvmzrefurkr4pdf74lgp3 ← your TAR address (yours will be different)
💡 Tip Copy and save your wallet address somewhere safe. Also back up your 24-word seed phrase — it is the only way to recover your wallet if your computer is lost.
2
Access Your ASIC Miner Settings

Every ASIC miner has a built-in web interface you access through your browser.

1. Plug your miner into your router with an ethernet cable
2. Power on the miner and wait ~2 minutes for it to boot
3. Find the miner's IP address — check your router admin page (usually 192.168.1.1)
4. Open your browser and go to that IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.100)
5. Login with default credentials:

Username: root Password: root (or) Username: admin Password: admin
3
Configure Pool Settings on Your Miner

Go to Miner Configuration → Pool Settings and enter the following:

FieldValue to Enter
Pool URL (Stratum) stratum+tcp://stratum.tarcoin.org:3333
Worker / Username Your TAR wallet address (e.g. tar1q...)
Password Anything — type x

Click Save & Apply. The miner will restart and connect to the pool automatically.

⚠️ Important Use YOUR OWN wallet address as the username. If you use someone else's address, your TAR rewards will go to them — not you.
4
Verify Your Miner is Working

After saving settings, check the miner's dashboard — you should see:

  • ✅ Pool status: Connected
  • ✅ Hashrate showing (e.g. 100 TH/s)
  • ✅ Shares accepted (increasing over time)

Also check the pool dashboard:

https://pool.tarcoin.org

Enter your TAR wallet address to see your live hashrate, shares, and balance.

5
Receive TAR Rewards

The mining pool automatically pays out TAR to your wallet address when you reach the minimum payout threshold. Open your tarcoin-qt wallet to see your balance.

Payouts happen automatically — no action required on your part.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to download anything from GitHub?
No. Just download the wallet from tarcoin.org/download.
Do I need to run my own blockchain node?
No. The mining pool handles the blockchain connection for you.
Can I use my Bitcoin ASIC miner?
Yes. Tarcoin uses SHA-256 — the same algorithm as Bitcoin. Any Bitcoin ASIC works.
When do I receive my TAR?
Automatically when your balance reaches the minimum payout threshold. Check pool.tarcoin.org for your stats.
What if the main pool is down?
The pool server at stratum.tarcoin.org is load-balanced across multiple servers for high availability. If you experience a connection issue, simply restart your miner — it will reconnect automatically.
What is the block reward?
50,000 TAR per block. Halving every 400,000 blocks (~7.6 years). Total supply: 50 billion TAR.
