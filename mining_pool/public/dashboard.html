Hashrate is measured from your share deltas, priced at the lowest difficulty the pool reported during the window. The pool publishes one difficulty per payout address while summing every rig on it, so an uncalibrated figure is indicative and a wide difficulty spread is shown as a range. Calibrate against your own miner for an exact number.

Luck compares blocks found against blocks your measured hashrate should have produced at the network difficulty. It is meaningless over minutes and only settles over days.

± is Poisson error on share arrival (1/√N) and covers sampling noise only.
